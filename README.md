**Vice Panel** turns your own photos into GTA-poster-style cover art: a multi-panel collage with white poster gutters, a hot two-tone title with a gold rule and an edition chip, and a sunset-or-neon colour grade — exported as a share-ready PNG. It is a static web app that runs entirely offline in a desktop browser; your photos are read on your own machine and never uploaded anywhere.

Four bundled samples (two photographs and two scenes painted in code — a neon downtown street and a golden-hour boulevard) fill the panels until you add your own.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. No build step, no install, no network.

## Features

- Six panel layouts — Showcase (wide hero over 3-over-2), Hero Poster, Cover Story, Duo Split, Quad Grid and Full Bleed
- Your photos in any panel: multi-file upload, a sample tray, click a panel to select it, drag to pan, scroll or slide to zoom
- Per-panel colour filters: Sunset, Neon, Noir, Chrome and Duotone
- Shuffle the panel assignment or swap two panels when a composition is almost right
- Poster title block: two-tone gradient display lettering with a neon halo, letterspaced subtitle, gold rule and a "VOL. 01 · LEONIDA" chip
- A global colour grade plus optional film grain and vignette
- Painted fallback scenes, so an empty panel still looks like poster art
- One-click PNG exports at 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 and 1920 × 1080

Everything is original: the lettering, scenes and layout are drawn in code, and no Rockstar Games artwork, logos or fonts are used.
